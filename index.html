03 · Research publications

Two papers on LLM behavior.

IEEE ICCISCSManufactured Neutrality: Auditing Latent Inference Bias in Large Language ModelsShivang Rai, Sachin GuptaUnder review
  • Built a 5-metric framework to audit identity-conditioned bias across 5 matched themes.
  • Found that alignment relocates bias rather than removing it, with Gemini's refusals bypassed by rephrasing alone.
IEEEExpected Calibration Error of Token-Probability Confidence in Open-Weight LLMs on SimpleQAShivang Rai, Sheen Ambardar, Om Tripathi, Sachin GuptaUnder review
  • Benchmarked 5 open-weight LLMs on 1,100 SimpleQA questions (ECE, Brier, AUROC, bootstrap CIs).
  • Found every model overconfident (ECE 0.47–0.67), with near-chance confidence ranking.
